The first release of Brook, CozyRSS rebuilt from the ground up as a native, local desktop app for macOS and Linux.

- **Local and private.** No accounts and no server. Your library is one SQLite file on your computer.
- **Follow anything.** RSS, Atom, JSON Feed, and YouTube channels. Paste a site and Brook finds its feed, with a preview before you follow.
- **Calm reading.** A typeset column with images, quotes, code, and video cards, plus reader mode for feeds that only send summaries.
- **Organized.** Today, Starred, Read later, folders, search across everything, and OPML import and export.
- **Discover.** A built-in catalog of more than a hundred hand-picked feeds.
- **Keyboard first.** J and K to move, S to star, L to save, V for reader mode.
- **Light, dark, and glass.** Follows the system; on macOS in dark mode, the window is soft glass.
- **Signed updates.** Brook checks GitHub once a day and verifies every download before it installs.
